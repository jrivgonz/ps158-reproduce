Get Out the Vote

A canvasser has 25 moves before the polls close. Two houses are supporters (🙂), one is not (😠). Knock on a supporter's door and they go vote. Can a computer learn the route by trial and error?

1. Play the game

Moves left Supporters turned out Score

Keys: arrows to move, space or K to knock. Moving and knocking each use one move. You must stand on a house to knock on its door.

2. Points for each move

These numbers come from reward() in the code. Change them to change what the agent is trying to do. Every move pays the move cost plus the item for what happened.

Changes apply to the game and to future training. An agent already trained on the old numbers keeps what it learned until you press Reset agent.

3. The Q-learning agent

The agent keeps a table with one number for every (state, action) pair: its estimate of the future points from taking that action there. It starts at 0 everywhere. While training it picks a random action 20% of the time (exploring) and its best-looking action otherwise, then nudges the table toward what actually happened.

Each point averages 50 training games. Training games include the 20% random exploring, so they score a bit lower than Watch it play.
